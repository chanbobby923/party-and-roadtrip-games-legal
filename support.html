How do lives work?

You start with five lives. Each game uses one, and one comes back every 30 minutes. Tap the hearts at the top of the home screen to watch a short ad for an extra life, or to unlock unlimited play.

I bought Unlock forever. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, connect to the internet, then tap Restore purchases in Settings or on the lives sheet.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every game works offline. Watching an ad and buying or restoring the unlock need a connection.

What about the adult categories?

Spicy, Unhinged, and NSFW 18+ stay off until you turn them on in a game. Turn them on only if everyone playing is 18 or older.

生命如何運作？

開始時有五條生命，每局用一條，每 30 分鐘回復一條。點按主畫面頂部的心形圖示，即可觀看短片廣告多得一條生命，或解鎖無限遊玩。

我已購買「永久解鎖」，換新手機後如何取回？

請以同一個 Apple 帳戶登入並連接網絡，然後在「設定」或生命頁面點按「恢復購買」。

如何申請退款？

所有購買均由 Apple 處理。請前往 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請退款。

需要網絡連線嗎？

不需要。所有遊戲均可離線遊玩。觀看廣告，以及購買或恢復解鎖時則需要連線。

成人類別如何處理？

「火辣」、「荒誕」及「成人 18+」在你於遊戲中開啟前一直維持關閉。請僅在所有玩家均已年滿 18 歲時開啟。
